Commercial Kitchen Equipment We Repair in West Memphis

Commercial Oven Repair

An oven that will not ignite, drifts away from its setpoint, or stops heating after startup needs a sequence-based diagnosis. We trace the call for heat through interlocks, ignition, flame proving, and temperature control, then test the igniter, flame sensor, gas valve, burners, thermostat or probe, elements, contactors, and convection fan as the model requires.

Commercial Fryer Service

For slow recovery, repeated high-limit trips, or a pilot that drops out, we compare the oil temperature with the controller reading and test the operating thermostat or probe, safety circuit, ignition parts, burners, and gas supply. We also check oil level and filtration flow so a circulation problem is not mistaken for a heating failure.

Commercial Dishwasher Repair

When a dishwasher fills slowly, leaves food soil, will not drain, or misses sanitizing temperature, we inspect the incoming water supply, fill valve and level controls, wash pump, strainers, spray arms, drain components, tank heat, and booster circuit. On chemical-sanitizing machines, the feed tubing and concentration are checked separately.

Ice Machine Repair & Cleaning

Low production, hollow or thin ice, long freeze cycles, and failed harvests can begin with water flow, scale, airflow, refrigeration, or controls. We review inlet and distribution water, pump operation, evaporator ice pattern, condenser condition, fan operation, cycle timing, harvest controls, and bin controls before recommending parts or cleaning.

Commercial Refrigeration Service

For a warm reach-in, iced evaporator, or compressor that cycles too frequently, we record cabinet temperature and inspect loading and airflow, door gaskets, condenser and evaporator coils, fan motors, defrost components, and temperature controls. Refrigerant-circuit testing follows only when those checks point to a sealed-system fault.

Griddles, Ranges & Holding Equipment

Griddle cold zones, weak range flames, and steam tables or food warmers that cannot hold temperature each require different tests. Depending on the unit, we inspect thermostats, probes, heating elements, pilots, igniters, burners, ports, orifices, air shutters, gas supply, switches, wiring, and water-pan condition.

Can you diagnose a commercial oven or fryer that will not heat correctly?

Yes. We compare actual temperature with the control setting and test the ignition or electric heating sequence, operating control, safety circuit, burners or elements, and the electrical connections that carry the load. This separates calibration, airflow, and supply problems from failed heat or control components.
