What Terry works on

Hands-on diagnosis and repair of the equipment that keeps a food-service kitchen running:

  • Conveyor pizza ovens — Middleby Marshall WOW Series and Lincoln Impinger units, including belt drives, speed controllers, ignition, heating elements, and blower systems
