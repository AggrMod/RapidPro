Restaurant Equipment Maintenance Services in Arlington

Commercial Oven and Range Maintenance

Uneven baking, delayed ignition, temperature drift, and weak range flames often develop before a complete shutdown. We compare actual cavity temperature with the control setting, observe ignition and flame proving, inspect burners and ports, and test probes, thermostats, safety switches, contactors, elements, and convection fans according to the unit.

Commercial Fryer Maintenance

Fryer service starts with recovery time and an independent oil-temperature reading. We inspect burners, pilots or electronic ignition, temperature probes, operating controls, high limits, drain valves, and filtration flow. That process helps distinguish a heating fault from restricted oil circulation, incorrect oil level, or a control that has drifted out of calibration.

Commercial Dishwasher Maintenance

For poor cleaning or inconsistent sanitizing, we check fill level, incoming water, wash-pump operation, strainers, spray arms, rinse flow, drain components, tank heat, and booster temperature. Chemical-sanitizing machines also require separate inspection of pickup tubing, pumps, and concentration so mechanical wash problems are not confused with chemical-feed issues.

Ice Machine Maintenance and Cleaning

We evaluate freeze and harvest cycles instead of treating low ice production as a cleaning-only problem. Maintenance can include inspecting the inlet valve, water distribution, pump, evaporator ice pattern, scale accumulation, condenser airflow, fan operation, harvest controls, and bin control. Refrigeration testing follows when cycle observations point to a sealed-system issue.

Commercial Refrigeration Maintenance

Reach-ins, prep tables, and other commercial refrigerators are checked with recorded cabinet temperatures, not touch alone. We inspect door gaskets, product loading and airflow, condenser and evaporator coils, fan motors, defrost components, drains, and temperature controls before deciding whether electrical readings or refrigerant-circuit measurements are necessary.

Preventative Maintenance Planning

A useful maintenance schedule reflects operating hours, heat and grease exposure, water scale, condenser location, and each unit's service history. We can document equipment condition, identify developing wear or sanitation concerns, and recommend practical inspection intervals so urgent findings can be separated from work that can be planned.

What does oven and fryer preventative maintenance include?

We verify temperature performance and recovery, observe ignition or electric heating sequences, and inspect operating controls, safety circuits, burners or elements, probes, wiring, and airflow components as applicable. Fryer maintenance can also include the drain and filtration system because restricted oil flow can affect recovery and cooking consistency.

How do you maintain dishwashers, ice machines, and refrigeration equipment?

Dishwasher maintenance covers fill, wash, drain, heat, rinse, and sanitizing systems. Ice-machine and refrigeration maintenance can include water-flow, scale, temperature, airflow, electrical, defrost, and cycle checks, with sealed-system testing used when those findings indicate a refrigeration-circuit problem.

How often should Arlington restaurant equipment receive preventative maintenance?

The right interval depends on operating hours, grease exposure, water quality, ambient heat, condenser location, and equipment type. High-use cooking equipment, scaled ice machines, and refrigeration near a cook line may benefit from quarterly checks; an initial inspection can establish whether quarterly or semiannual service is appropriate for each unit.

Is same-day repair available if maintenance finds an equipment failure?

Same-day repair may be available based on technician routing, the failed component, and parts availability. Some problems can be corrected during the maintenance visit, while model-specific parts or additional diagnostic time may require a return appointment. We explain the finding and options before proceeding.
