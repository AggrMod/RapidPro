Our Bartlett Services

Commercial Oven Repair

An oven that runs cold, burns one rack, or takes too long to recover may have a temperature-control, airflow, ignition, or door-seal problem. We compare actual cavity temperature with the controls and test the thermostat or probe, fan motor, burners or elements, contactors, igniter, and gasket before replacing parts.

Commercial Fryer Service

For fryers that will not light, recover slowly, overheat, or trip the high-limit, we check the complete heat-control circuit. That can include the thermostat or temperature probe, high-limit, heating elements and contactors on electric units, or the pilot, thermopile, gas valve, and burners on gas models.

Dishwasher Repair

Greasy wares, a cold final rinse, poor draining, and water leaking at the door require different tests. We inspect the wash pump, spray arms, booster heater, temperature controls, fill and rinse valves, chemical feed, drain components, and door seals, then verify operating temperatures and water flow after the repair.

Ice Machine Maintenance

Ice machines lose production when scale restricts water distribution, a dirty condenser limits heat rejection, or the freeze and harvest controls fall out of sequence. We clean and sanitize accessible food-zone parts, inspect the water system and condenser, and test the inlet valve, pump, sensors, bin control, and harvest components.
