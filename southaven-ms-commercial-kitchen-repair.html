Commercial Kitchen Equipment We Repair in Southaven

Commercial Oven Repair

When an oven will not light, heats unevenly, or overshoots its setpoint, we check the ignition sequence, flame sensor or thermocouple, gas valve, burners, thermostat, temperature probe, and door seal. On electric ovens, testing also covers elements, contactors, and control voltage before parts are quoted.

Commercial Fryer Service

Slow recovery, a pilot that drops out, and nuisance high-limit trips do not all point to the same part. We compare actual oil temperature with the control reading, then inspect the probe or thermostat, high-limit circuit, ignition components, burners, gas supply, and filtration flow to isolate the fault.

Commercial Dishwasher Repair

For poor wash results or a machine that misses sanitizing temperature, we inspect incoming water, tank and booster heat, thermostats, contactors, wash-pump operation, spray arms, water-level controls, and drains. Low-temperature machines also require a check of the chemical feed and concentration.

Ice Machine Repair & Cleaning

Low production, thin ice, long harvests, and water leaks can start with scale, restricted water flow, a dirty condenser, failed fan, weak water pump, or a freeze or harvest control problem. We evaluate the complete ice-making cycle and sanitation condition instead of treating every symptom as a refrigeration failure.

Commercial Refrigeration Service

A warm reach-in or prep table may have blocked airflow, dirty coils, failed evaporator or condenser fans, damaged door gaskets, a defrost fault, or an inaccurate sensor. We record cabinet temperature and inspect the electrical and refrigeration systems before recommending sealed-system work.

Griddles, Ranges & Holding Equipment

We diagnose griddle cold spots, range burners with weak flame, and steam tables or warmers that cannot hold temperature. Checks may include burners and orifices, ignition parts, thermostats, heating elements, switches, wiring, and water-pan condition based on the equipment design.

Can you diagnose an oven or fryer that will not heat correctly?

Yes. For ovens and fryers, we test the temperature control and safety circuits along with the ignition system, burners, gas controls, or electric heating components used by that model. Actual temperature readings help distinguish a calibration problem from a failed control or heat-source problem.

Do you service commercial refrigeration and ice machines?

Yes. We diagnose reach-ins, prep tables, and commercial ice machines for temperature, airflow, water, electrical, defrost, and refrigeration-cycle problems. Ice-machine service can also include scale removal and cleaning when buildup is affecting production or sanitation.

How often should Southaven commercial kitchen equipment receive preventative maintenance?

The interval depends on operating hours, grease load, water quality, and equipment type. High-volume cooking equipment, ice machines exposed to scale, and refrigeration with grease-loaded coils may need quarterly attention, while lighter-use equipment may be suitable for semiannual service after an initial inspection.
