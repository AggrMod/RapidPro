Conveyor Pizza Oven Repair

On a conveyor oven, heat and bake time have to work together. When pizzas come out wrong or the belt stops, we check belt speed, the drive motor and speed controller, drive gears, blowers, air-finger placement, burners and ignition, and the safety interlock switches behind the panels. This is part of our commercial oven repair in Memphis.

Problems we fix on conveyor ovens

  • "Front Belt Jam" or "Rear Belt Jam" alert that comes back after the oven is cleared, which points to the speed controller and motor feedback rather than an obstruction.
  • Conveyor won't move with no jam alert, usually a broken gear in the belt drive.
  • Pizzas under- or over-cooked because the belt isn't running at the set bake time.
  • Oven won't reach temperature: flame sensor, ignition module, or the inducer motor and damper.
  • No display at all, often a panel interlock switch that isn't closing.
  • "High control compartment temperature" shutdowns from clogged fan filters.
  • Flame coming through a gap between burners instead of the burner holes. Turn the oven off and call; the burner has failed.

Before You Call

A few things are safe to check yourself and might get you running again: make sure the oven is plugged in and every panel is closed flush, clear anything visibly stuck in the belt with the oven off, check that the BLOWER and HEAT switches are on, and clean the front fan filters. If you smell gas, shut off the gas supply and get everyone out. When you call, tell us whether a jam alert is showing and whether changing the bake time made any difference; those two details tell the technician a lot before arriving.

Pizza Oven Repair Questions

My conveyor oven belt won't move, but there's no belt jam alert. What's wrong?

That usually points to a broken gear in the belt drive rather than a jam. The belt will move freely by hand, which is different from the machine refusing to run. Don't force anything. Call for service and mention there's no jam alert showing; that detail tells the technician it's likely mechanical before they walk in.

Why are my pizzas coming out under- or over-cooked?

Bake time is set on the oven's control panel, and you can adjust it in about 20% steps as a stopgap. If adjusting it changes the result, the motor's internal Hall effect sensor is often failing, and the motor (usually with the speed controller) will eventually need replacing. If adjusting it does nothing, the drive isn't responding at all. Either way, tell the technician which one you saw.

My conveyor oven won't reach temperature. What should I check?

First make sure both the BLOWER and HEAT switches are on; they are easy to bump. If they are on and it still won't heat, it needs a technician: the cause can be the flame sensor, the ignition module, or the inducer motor and its damper, and those take diagnostic tools to tell apart.

Do you repair Middleby Marshall and Lincoln Impinger conveyor ovens?

Yes. We work on Middleby Marshall and Lincoln Impinger conveyor ovens every week, and we have brand-specific troubleshooting guides for both. We also repair deck pizza ovens throughout Memphis and the surrounding area.
