Restaurant Equipment We Repair in Cordova

Commercial Oven Repair

An oven that will not start, heats unevenly, or drifts away from its setpoint needs a sequence-based diagnosis. We test ignition and flame-proving parts, gas valves and burners, or electric elements and contactors, then compare the thermostat or sensor reading with actual cavity temperature.

Commercial Fryer Service

When a fryer recovers slowly, will not light, or trips its high limit, we check oil temperature against the controller, inspect the probe or thermostat and safety circuit, and evaluate the pilot, igniter, flame sensor, burners, and gas supply. Filtration and oil-level problems are checked separately from heating faults.

Commercial Dishwasher Repair

A dishwasher can run through a cycle and still leave racks dirty or miss its sanitizing target. We inspect wash pressure, spray-arm condition, tank and booster heat, water-level controls, drains, contactors, and thermostats. On chemical-sanitizing models, we also verify the chemical feed.

Ice Machine Repair & Cleaning

For low ice production, malformed cubes, or a machine stuck in freeze or harvest, we inspect water flow, pump operation, scale buildup, bin controls, condenser airflow, fan operation, and cycle controls. Cleaning and refrigeration testing are handled as separate parts of the diagnosis.

Commercial Refrigeration Service

Warm cabinets, icing, and short cycling can result from dirty coils, failed fans, torn gaskets, blocked airflow, faulty defrost components, or inaccurate temperature controls. We document temperatures and test those systems before determining whether the sealed refrigeration circuit needs attention.

Griddles, Ranges & Holding Equipment

Cold zones on a griddle, unstable range flames, and steam tables that lose temperature each have different failure paths. Depending on the unit, we inspect burners, orifices, pilots, ignition controls, thermostats, heating elements, switches, wiring, and water-pan condition.

What commercial kitchen equipment do you repair in Cordova?

We service commercial ovens, fryers, griddles, ranges, dishwashers, ice machines, reach-in refrigerators, prep tables, steam tables, and holding equipment. Providing the manufacturer, model number, serial number, and exact symptom helps us prepare for the unit.

Can you repair a fryer or oven that keeps shutting down?

Yes. Repeated shutdowns can involve a flame-sensing or ignition fault, a weak thermocouple, an opening high-limit circuit, unstable gas supply, a failing temperature control, or an electrical connection that opens under heat. We test the operating and safety sequence to identify where the shutdown begins.

Do you diagnose restaurant dishwashers that are not sanitizing?

Yes. We verify actual wash and final-rinse temperatures, booster-heater operation, water level, wash pressure, and the thermostats and contactors that control heat. For low-temperature machines, we also inspect the sanitizer feed rather than assuming the problem is the heater.

Is same-day commercial kitchen repair available in Cordova?

Same-day appointments may be available based on call volume, technician routing, the type of failure, and parts availability. Call as early as possible and share any error code, current equipment temperature, model and serial number, and whether the unit can be shut down safely.

When should a Cordova restaurant schedule preventative maintenance?

Maintenance frequency should match use and operating conditions. High-volume fryers and ovens, refrigeration near grease-producing equipment, and ice machines affected by hard-water scale often benefit from quarterly checks. An initial inspection can determine whether quarterly or semiannual service fits each unit.
