Salamander Broiler and Charbroiler Problems We Repair

Radiant & Lava Rock Charbroilers

We trace cold zones and weak flames to clogged burner ports, damaged burners, shifted radiants, grease buildup, or incorrect gas pressure. Radiants and briquettes also need to be seated correctly before the heat will spread evenly across the grate.

Infrared Charbroiler Service

Infrared units depend on intact ceramic emitters and a clean, even burner flame. We inspect cracked or plugged tiles, ignition components, air shutters, orifices, and regulators to identify the cause of low heat.

Salamander Pilot & Ignition Repair

A pilot that will not light or stay lit may have a blocked pilot orifice, weak thermocouple, dirty flame sensor, failed spark igniter, or a safety valve that is not proving flame. We test the ignition sequence and gas controls before replacing parts.

Gas Valve, Burner & Control Repair

We service sticking burner valves, broken knobs, slow-opening safety valves, damaged burner assemblies, and loose control wiring. On adjustable salamanders, we also inspect the rack, carriage, and heat-position controls for safe movement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you repair both commercial salamander broilers and charbroilers?

Yes. We diagnose countertop and range-mounted salamanders along with radiant, lava rock, ceramic briquette, and infrared charbroilers. When you call, have the manufacturer, model number, and fuel type ready so we can prepare for the unit on your line.

Why is my commercial charbroiler heating unevenly?

Uneven heat usually comes from clogged burner ports, grease or carbon buildup, damaged burners, radiants or briquettes sitting out of position, cracked infrared tiles, or low gas pressure. The grate may look clean while buildup below it is disrupting the flame pattern.

Why will my salamander broiler pilot not stay lit?

A dirty pilot orifice, weak thermocouple, misaligned flame sensor, failing safety valve, or unstable gas pressure can shut the pilot down. If the unit uses electronic ignition, the igniter, flame-sensing circuit, and wiring also need to be checked.

Is infrared charbroiler repair different from radiant charbroiler repair?

Yes. A radiant charbroiler transfers burner heat through metal radiants, rock, or briquettes. An infrared unit uses ceramic emitters that can crack or become plugged. The flame, air mixture, and gas pressure matter on both designs, but their heat-transfer parts fail differently.

What causes yellow flames or soot on a commercial broiler?

Yellow flames and soot point to incomplete combustion. Common causes include restricted burner ports, a blocked air shutter, the wrong orifice, incorrect gas pressure, or a burner that is not seated correctly. Stop using the unit if you smell gas or see heavy soot and call for service.

How often should a commercial salamander or charbroiler be serviced?

Most units should be inspected at least every six months. Busy charbroilers that collect grease and carbon quickly may need quarterly service. Daily grate cleaning helps, but the burners, pilots, radiants, valves, and air openings below the cooking surface also need periodic inspection.
