Commercial Appliances We Repair in Olive Branch

Commercial Oven Repair

For long preheat times, uneven baking, or an oven that starts and then shuts down, we follow the heating sequence from the call for heat through ignition and flame proving. Testing can include door interlocks, igniters, flame sensors, gas valves, burners, thermostats, probes, elements, contactors, and fan operation based on the model.

Commercial Fryer Service

When oil temperature falls behind demand, the pilot will not hold, or the high limit opens repeatedly, we compare an independent temperature reading with the controller and test the operating thermostat or probe, safety circuit, ignition components, burners, and gas supply. Oil level and filtration problems are evaluated separately from the heat source.

Commercial Dishwasher Repair

A slow fill, weak wash pattern, standing water, or low final-rinse temperature requires more than a cycle check. We inspect strainers and spray arms, measure wash-pump operation, test fill and level controls, check drain components, and verify tank or booster heat. Chemical-sanitizing models also receive a feed-system check.

Ice Machine Repair & Cleaning

Thin cubes, low daily yield, water leaking into the bin, and extended freeze or harvest cycles can come from different systems. We review cycle timing, inlet and distribution water, float and pump operation, evaporator ice pattern, harvest and bin controls, condenser airflow, and scale accumulation before identifying the repair.

Commercial Refrigeration Service

For a warm reach-in, iced evaporator, or compressor that cycles too often, we document cabinet temperature and inspect product loading, door gaskets, airflow, coils, fan motors, defrost parts, and temperature controls. Refrigerant-circuit testing follows when those checks indicate a sealed-system problem.

Griddles, Ranges & Broilers

Cold griddle zones, weak range flames, and charbroilers that soot or heat unevenly call for equipment-specific combustion and temperature checks. Depending on the unit, we inspect pilots, igniters, burners, ports, orifices, air shutters, radiants, thermostats, gas pressure, switches, and wiring.

What commercial kitchen appliances do you repair in Olive Branch?

We service commercial ovens, fryers, griddles, ranges, broilers, dishwashers, ice machines, reach-in refrigerators, prep tables, steam tables, and holding equipment. Sharing the manufacturer, model number, serial number, and exact symptom helps us prepare for the correct unit.

Can you diagnose an oven or fryer that heats inconsistently?

Yes. We compare actual temperature with the control setting and test the ignition or electric heating sequence, temperature sensor or thermostat, safety circuit, burners or elements, and the connections that carry the load. That process separates calibration and airflow issues from failed heat or control components.

Do you repair commercial dishwashers that leave dishes dirty or wet?

Yes. We check wash pressure, pump operation, spray arms, water level, drains, tank heat, and final-rinse or booster temperature. We also look at rinse-aid and sanitizer delivery when the machine design uses those chemicals, because wash-quality and drying complaints are not always caused by a heater.

Is same-day commercial appliance repair available in Olive Branch?

Same-day appointments may be available based on call volume, technician routing, the equipment involved, and parts availability. Call early with the model and serial number, current temperature or error code, and a clear description of when the failure began so we can assess the request.

How often should Olive Branch restaurant equipment receive preventative maintenance?

The right interval depends on operating hours, grease exposure, water scale, ambient temperature, and equipment type. High-use cooking appliances, ice machines with mineral buildup, and refrigeration near a cook line may benefit from quarterly checks; an initial inspection can establish whether quarterly or semiannual service fits each unit.
