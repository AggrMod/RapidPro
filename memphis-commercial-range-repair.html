Commercial Gas Range Repair

Weak burners, yellow flames, pilots that will not hold, and an oven base that will not maintain temperature do not all point to the same part. We inspect burner ports and air shutters, pilot assemblies, thermocouples or thermopiles, safety valves, thermostats, and gas controls before recommending a repair. Ranges are part of our commercial cooking equipment repair in Memphis; for the oven side, see commercial oven repair.

Range and stove problems we fix

  • Pilot won't stay lit — thermocouple, pilot orifice or gas safety valve.
  • Weak, uneven or yellow burner flames — burner ports, air shutters, gas supply or controls.
  • Burner won't light on one or more positions.
  • Range oven won't hold temperature — thermostat, oven burner, pilot or safety valve.
  • You smell gas — shut off the gas supply, get everyone out, and call. Don't try to fix a gas leak yourself.

Range & Stove Repair Questions

Why won't the pilot on my commercial range stay lit?

A pilot that won't stay lit is usually a faulty thermocouple, a clogged pilot orifice, or a defective gas safety valve. Don't keep relighting it or run equipment with gas problems; call (901) 257-9417.

Why are my range burner flames yellow or weak?

Weak or yellow flames can come from dirty or blocked burner ports, air shutters out of adjustment, or a gas supply or control problem. We inspect the burner ports and air shutters, pilot assemblies, safety valves and gas controls before recommending a repair.

The oven under my range won't hold temperature. Is that the same problem?

Not necessarily. A range oven that won't maintain temperature points to the thermostat, the oven burner or pilot, the thermocouple or thermopile, or the safety valve, so we test those separately from the top burners.

Do you repair Vulcan, Garland and Wolf ranges?

Yes. We repair commercial gas ranges, including Vulcan, Garland and Wolf, for restaurants and food service kitchens in Memphis and the surrounding area.
